Qurix EMR

Package: com.qurix.emr

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how iHUB Technologies Private Limited ("iHUB", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Qurix EMR mobile application (the "App"), package name com.qurix.emr, and related services operated under the Qurix brand.

Qurix EMR is an electronic medical records (EMR) application intended for authorised healthcare professionals and hospital staff. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to:

  • Healthcare professionals, nurses, and hospital staff who log in to the App.
  • Organisations (hospitals, clinics, or healthcare providers) that subscribe to Qurix services.

The App is not intended for use by patients as end users. Patient health information may be accessed and processed through the App by authorised clinical users on behalf of their healthcare organisation.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following categories of information:

3.1 Account and authentication information

  • Username and login credentials submitted through our single sign-on (SSO) service.
  • Access tokens, refresh tokens, and session identifiers used to keep you signed in securely.
  • Organisation, location, role, and permission details associated with your account.

3.2 Professional user information

  • Name, employee ID, designation, mobile number, and profile details of authorised users.
  • Department, location, and role-based access configuration.

3.3 Patient and clinical health information

When you use EMR features, the App may access, display, or transmit patient-related health information, including but not limited to:

  • Patient demographics and visit details.
  • Vitals, prescriptions, medications, investigations, and treatment records.
  • Medical history, physical examination notes, and discharge summaries.
  • Nursing worklists, inpatient records, and appointment information.
  • ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) related data when ABHA features are enabled.

This information is processed on behalf of your healthcare organisation and in accordance with applicable healthcare laws and your organisation's policies.

3.4 Technical and usage information

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • IP address, network connection status, and timestamps of access.
  • Logs related to authentication, errors, and system performance for security and support.
  • Temporary data stored locally on your device during an active session (for example, authentication helpers and selected workspace context).

4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Authenticate users and provide secure access to the App.
  • Enable clinical workflows such as doctor visits, nursing tasks, inpatient management, and discharge documentation.
  • Display, record, and synchronise patient health information within your organisation's EMR system.
  • Support ABHA and ABDM integrations where enabled by your organisation.
  • Maintain security, prevent unauthorised access, and investigate incidents.
  • Provide technical support and improve reliability of the App and related services.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

5. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information or patient health information.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your healthcare organisation: Clinical and user data is accessible to authorised personnel within your organisation according to role-based permissions.
  • With service providers: We use trusted infrastructure and technology partners to host, secure, and operate Qurix services (including authentication, cloud hosting, and content delivery).
  • With government health systems: When ABHA/ABDM features are used, relevant data may be exchanged with authorised government health infrastructure as required for those integrations.
  • For legal reasons: When required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • With your consent: Where additional sharing is requested and permitted by applicable law.

Key domains and services used by the App include:

  • qurix.io – core application and API services
  • sso.qurix.ai – authentication services
  • bi.qurix.io – reporting and analytics (where enabled)
  • ABDM/ABHA government gateway services (where enabled)

6. Data Storage and Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted communication over HTTPS, access controls, authentication mechanisms, and role-based permissions.

Some session-related data may be stored temporarily on your device while you are logged in. Primary clinical records are stored on secure servers operated or managed by iHUB and/or your healthcare organisation.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the App and related services, fulfil the purposes described in this policy, and comply with legal and regulatory requirements. Clinical records are retained according to your healthcare organisation's policies and applicable healthcare record-keeping laws.

Session and authentication data on your device is generally cleared when you log out or when the session expires.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where applicable), you may have rights to access, correct, update, or request deletion of certain personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.

Because Qurix EMR is typically provided through your employer or healthcare organisation, requests relating to your professional account or patient records may need to be submitted through your organisation's designated administrator, in addition to contacting us using the details below.

9. Permissions Used by the App

The Android version of Qurix EMR requests the following permission:

  • Internet access – required to connect to Qurix servers, authenticate users, and synchronise clinical data.

The App does not request access to device contacts, camera, microphone, or location unless such permissions are added in a future version. If that changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by authorised healthcare professionals and is not directed at children under the age of 18 for direct registration or use. Patient records relating to minors may be processed by authorised clinical users as part of legitimate healthcare services.

11. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located in India or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. Where required, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such processing.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact Us

Qurix Health (Patient)

Package: com.qurix.patient

Operated by: iHUB Technologies Private Limited

1. Introduction

Qurix Health is a patient-facing healthcare application operated by iHUB Technologies Private Limited ("iHUB", "Qurix", "we", "us", or "our"), package name com.qurix.patient.

Qurix Health connects patients with healthcare providers and healthcare facilities using the Qurix Hospital Management System (HMS). Depending on the services enabled by a healthcare facility, the application allows patients to access relevant healthcare information, manage appointments, exchange messages and medical documents, receive prescriptions, make payments where enabled, and participate in live video consultations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information when you use Qurix Health. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to:

  • Patients and individuals who register or sign in to the App using a mobile number and OTP.
  • Caregivers or family members who use the App on behalf of a patient, where permitted.

The App is intended for patients and their authorised caregivers. Clinical staff use separate Qurix applications (such as Qurix EMR or SuperDoc - For Doctors). Healthcare organisations that provide services through Qurix remain responsible for their own clinical and operational policies. The availability of information and features may vary by healthcare facility.

3. Information We Collect and Process

The information processed depends on the features you use and the services provided by your healthcare facility.

3.1 Account and contact information

  • Name, mobile number, email address (where provided), and account identifiers.
  • Authentication information, including OTP verification and session tokens.
  • Information needed to associate your account with a healthcare provider or facility in the Qurix HMS.

3.2 Health information and medical documents

When you use Qurix Health, we may process health information obtained from the Qurix HMS or submitted by you or your healthcare provider, including:

  • Appointment and consultation-related information.
  • Medical reports and other healthcare documents.
  • Prescriptions shared by your healthcare provider.
  • Messages and attachments exchanged through patient–doctor chat.
  • Images or files you choose to upload (for example, a photograph of a laboratory report).
  • Other healthcare information required to provide the features you use.

Healthcare information displayed in Qurix Health may originate from records maintained by the healthcare facility in its Qurix HMS.

3.3 Payment information

  • Order identifiers, amount, currency, and payment status for consultations, lab reports, or other billable services (where enabled).
  • Payment gateway transaction references (for example, via Razorpay) needed to confirm successful payment.

Card, UPI, and other sensitive payment instrument details are collected and processed by the payment gateway provider. We do not store your full card number or UPI PIN in the App.

3.4 Device and technical information

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • IP address, network connection status, and timestamps of access.
  • Logs related to authentication, errors, payments, and system performance for security and support.
  • Push notification tokens used to deliver appointment, chat, or call alerts when you enable notifications.
  • Temporary data stored locally on your device during an active session (for example, secure tokens and selected visit context).

4. Camera, Microphone, Photo Library, and Document Access

Qurix Health requests access to device features when needed to provide the relevant functionality.

4.1 Photo Library

If you choose to upload a photo, Qurix Health may request access to your photo library so that you can select an image to share with your healthcare provider.

For example, you can select a photograph of a laboratory report or prescription and upload it through chat so your doctor can review it.

Photo access is used only to support the image-selection and upload functionality you request. The App does not access your photo library in the background or for unrelated purposes.

4.2 Documents and files

Qurix Health allows you to select documents using the device's document picker. For example, you can select a medical report stored in your device's files and share it with your healthcare provider through chat.

4.3 Camera and microphone

Qurix Health uses your camera and microphone to enable live video consultations with healthcare providers. You can manage applicable device permissions through your device settings. If you deny permission, the associated feature may not be available.

5. Live Video Consultations

Qurix Health supports live video consultations between patients and healthcare providers using LiveKit Cloud, a managed video infrastructure service.

During a consultation, audio and video are transmitted to facilitate real-time communication. Technical connection information may also be processed to establish and maintain the session.

Qurix Health does not record video consultations or store their audio or video content. This statement refers to Qurix Health's consultation workflow and does not mean that technical connection metadata is never processed by the services involved.

Medical documents, messages, and prescriptions exchanged separately through the application are handled according to the relevant sections of this Privacy Policy.

6. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information, as applicable, to:

  • Create and manage user accounts and authenticate you securely using OTP.
  • Connect patients with their healthcare providers.
  • Retrieve and display relevant information from the Qurix HMS.
  • Facilitate appointments and live video consultations.
  • Enable patient–doctor chat and the exchange of medical documents and images.
  • Allow healthcare providers to share prescriptions and consultation-related information.
  • Process payments for services and confirm payment status (where enabled).
  • Deliver push notifications related to your care journey when enabled.
  • Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the application.
  • Protect the security and reliability of our services and investigate misuse.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal health information to advertisers or use it for targeted advertising.

7. How Information Is Shared

We share information as necessary to provide the services described in this Policy, subject to applicable law.

7.1 Healthcare providers and facilities

Information relevant to your healthcare interactions may be made available to the healthcare provider or facility associated with your account. This may include appointments, messages, uploaded documents, prescriptions, and other relevant healthcare information. Access is subject to the application's functionality and applicable authorisation arrangements.

7.2 Service providers

We may use service providers to support hosting, application infrastructure, video consultations, communications, payments, technical operations, and security.

Qurix Health currently uses LiveKit Cloud to facilitate live video consultations. Information necessary to establish and maintain a consultation may be processed by the relevant service provider. Payment-related details may be shared with payment gateways (such as Razorpay) as needed to complete and verify transactions.

Service providers are expected to process information in accordance with applicable contractual obligations and relevant privacy and security requirements.

7.3 Legal and regulatory requirements

We may disclose information where required or permitted by applicable law, a valid legal process, or a lawful request from a competent authority.

7.4 Business transfers

If Qurix undergoes a merger, restructuring, or transfer of business assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

Key domains and services used by the App include:

  • qurix.io – core application and API services
  • sso.qurix.ai – authentication services
  • Razorpay – payment processing (where enabled)
  • LiveKit Cloud – real-time video and audio for teleconsultations (where enabled)
  • Push notification services (Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging, where enabled)

8. Data Storage and Security

Qurix stores chat attachments, uploaded documents, and prescriptions in Qurix-controlled application, database, or file-storage infrastructure.

We implement administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse. These measures include encrypted communication over HTTPS, access controls, authentication mechanisms, and secure on-device storage for session credentials where supported by the device platform.

Access to information is restricted to authorised users and systems that require it for legitimate operational or healthcare purposes. Some session-related data may be stored temporarily on your device while you are logged in. Primary health and administrative records may also be maintained in the healthcare facility's Qurix HMS.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the relevant services, maintain application functionality and security, and comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations. Retention periods may differ for account information, messages, uploaded documents, prescriptions, payment records, and technical logs.

Qurix Health currently does not provide a self-service account-deletion feature within the application. You may contact us using the details in Section 13 to submit a request relating to your account or personal information. Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law and the relevant account-management arrangements.

A Qurix Health app account and the medical records maintained by a healthcare facility are distinct. A request concerning your app account does not automatically delete clinical records maintained in the Qurix HMS by your healthcare facility. Such records may need to be retained where required by applicable law or healthcare-record obligations. Where information must be retained, it will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Session and authentication data on your device is generally cleared when you log out or when the session expires.

10. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where applicable), you may have rights to access, correct, update, or request deletion of certain personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.

You may sign out of the App at any time. You can also manage camera, microphone, photo library, and notification permissions through your device settings. Because some health records are maintained by your hospital or clinic, requests relating to clinical records may need to be submitted through your healthcare organisation's designated administrator, in addition to contacting us using the details below.

To exercise applicable privacy rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. We may request information necessary to verify your identity and process your request securely.

11. Children's Privacy

Where Qurix Health is used by or on behalf of a child, personal information will be processed in accordance with applicable laws, including requirements relating to parental or guardian consent where applicable. We do not knowingly allow children under the age of 18 to create an independent account without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. Parents or lawful guardians may contact us regarding a child's personal information or applicable privacy rights.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our application, services, data practices, service providers, or applicable legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
